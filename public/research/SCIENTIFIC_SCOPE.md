# Scientific Scope and Study Outcomes

The research viewer displays frozen evidence. It does not solve research hypotheses, optimize sections or generate new candidates. Source IDs, lineage maps, load definitions, engine labels and mesh distinctions remain explicit.

## Controlled H0–H3 hypotheses

H0 is the reference Bridge. H1 changes 70 hidden-chord node positions and 580 incident member geometries. H2 removes 360 internal braces with unchanged source coordinates. H3 removes 496 H0 members and adds 26 vertical hangers; 2,601 frame members and 84 cables remain. H3 v1.1 corrects descriptive metadata only; archived numerical responses retain their v1 model identity and the metadata erratum.

Full-Bridge results use prescribed pressure/prestress and exclude structural self-weight. H0–H2 prestress-only fields do not have complete native web-coordinate verification: only qualified summaries are available. A pressure-loaded agreement does not remove that limitation. Comparison tables distinguish total displacement from the pointwise loaded-minus-prestress increment.

## Idealized B1+ final26

B1+ is a deterministic material-allocation study. The 36 m substructure contains 14 source nodes and 19 physical members inherited from H3. Idealized restraints are new boundary assumptions, not inherited full-Bridge connections. Source-node identity is distinct from refined numerical-mesh nodes.

The final26 study achieved **FULL COMPUTATIONAL PASS**, a successful computational, gravity-aware, member-screened **2.7658% modeled tube-mass reduction**. Only members **1520/1538** change wall thickness from **26.404 to 21.404 mm** at unchanged **640 mm outer diameter**. Geometry does not change between the qualified uniform comparator and the candidate. The original H3 transverse-member wall was 28 mm; it is a different reference state.

The modeled tube mass changes from 78,149.07868363462 to 75,987.63571417955 kg. Local terminal response increases remain visible: the representative displacement changes from 6.833027 to 8.001523 mm and the stress diagnostic from 61.608804 to 72.119236 MPa. These local comparisons do not substitute for the full reserved response screens. All 26 records, including the intentional lower-comparator FAIL, are retained. The study includes configuration-dependent gravity, 8 independent Euler–Bernoulli pairs, 12 mesh comparisons and 4 native Euler–Bernoulli/Timoshenko comparisons. Independent Euler–Bernoulli checks do not establish an independent full-system Timoshenko validation.

## Interpretation limits

These results concern idealized computational models. They do not demonstrate a global optimum, full-Bridge mass reduction, code compliance, complete connection/fabrication performance or physical engineering validation. Missing evidence is never represented as zero, a live prediction or a successful verification.
